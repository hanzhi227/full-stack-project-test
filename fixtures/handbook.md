# Northstar employee handbook

This is a fictional handbook for testing. It contains no real employee information.

## Expense reimbursement

Before requesting reimbursement, obtain your manager's written approval and keep an itemized receipt.
Submit the expense form within 30 calendar days of the purchase. Include the receipt and written approval.
Finance reviews complete requests within five business days.
The policy code for reimbursement is EXP-042.

## Travel

Book travel only after your manager approves the trip in writing.
The daily meal allowance for business travel is $60. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## Time off

Request planned time off at least two weeks in advance through the time-off form.
Your manager must approve the request before you book personal travel.

## Equipment

Contact IT before purchasing a laptop or other work equipment.
IT supplies approved equipment. Personal purchases without IT approval are not reimbursable.
