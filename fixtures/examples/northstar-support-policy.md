# Northstar customer support policy

This is a fictional document for upload, embedding, and question-answering tests. All products, email addresses, and policies are made up.

Effective date: February 1, 2026
Owner: Customer Support

## Product and support channels

Northstar Atlas is a hosted project-management service. Customers can contact support at support@northstar.example or submit a ticket through the Atlas help menu.

Standard support hours are Monday through Friday, 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Eastern Time, excluding company holidays. This document does not list the company holiday calendar.

Enterprise customers also have access to a 24-hour emergency channel for Severity 1 incidents. The emergency channel is not available on the Starter or Team plan.

## Severity levels and first-response targets

A first-response target measures how quickly support acknowledges a ticket and begins investigating. It is not a promise that the issue will be resolved within that time.

| Severity | Definition | First-response target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Severity 1 | The production service is unavailable to all users in a customer workspace, with no workaround. | Enterprise: one clock hour, including nights and weekends. Starter and Team: four support hours. |
| Severity 2 | A major feature is unavailable, but the service remains usable or a workaround exists. | Four support hours on all plans. |
| Severity 3 | A minor defect, a how-to question, or a feature request. | Two business days on all plans. |

Support hours count only during standard support hours. Clock hours run continuously, including outside standard support hours.

A problem affecting only one user is normally Severity 2 or Severity 3, not Severity 1. Support may revise the severity after reviewing the impact.

## What to include in a ticket

Include your workspace ID, the affected feature, the time the problem began with its time zone, and steps to reproduce the issue. If possible, attach a screenshot with personal information removed.

Do not include passwords, authentication codes, payment-card details, or API secrets. Support will never ask you to send those values in a ticket.

## Escalation

Reply to the existing ticket if the impact increases or the first-response target has passed. Do not open a duplicate ticket for the same issue.

For an ongoing Severity 1 incident, the incident owner posts an update every 60 minutes until service is restored. A written incident summary is shared within five business days after restoration.

## Service credits

Only Enterprise customers are eligible for service credits under this policy. An eligible outage must last at least 60 consecutive minutes and make the production service unavailable to all users in the workspace.

Request a credit within 14 calendar days after the outage ends. Include the workspace ID and the related incident number. Approved credits equal 10% of the monthly subscription fee and are capped at 20% of that fee per calendar month. Credits apply to a future invoice and are not cash refunds.

Scheduled maintenance announced at least 48 hours in advance does not qualify. Problems caused by a customer's network, devices, or configuration also do not qualify.

## Example

An Enterprise workspace experiences a 90-minute production outage affecting every user, with no workaround. Support must first respond within one clock hour and provide updates every 60 minutes while the incident remains active. The customer may request a service credit within 14 calendar days after the outage ends. For a monthly subscription fee of $500, a single approved credit is $50, and total credits for that calendar month cannot exceed $100.
