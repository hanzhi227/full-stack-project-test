# Northstar employee onboarding guide

This is a fictional document for upload, embedding, and question-answering tests. All names, email addresses, and policies are made up.

Document ID: ONB-101
Effective date: January 15, 2026
Owner: People Operations

## Before your first day

People Operations sends your onboarding checklist five business days before your start date. Complete the checklist and return your signed employment agreement at least two business days before you start.

Your manager assigns an onboarding buddy. The buddy helps with everyday questions but does not approve expenses, access requests, or time off.

For onboarding questions, email people@northstar.example.

## Your first day

New employees attend a remote welcome session at 9:30 a.m. Eastern Time. The session lasts 45 minutes and covers the company directory, communication tools, and the first-week schedule.

IT schedules a separate 30-minute equipment setup session. IT supplies an approved laptop and a security key. Contact IT before buying any work equipment; personal equipment purchases without IT approval are not reimbursable.

For equipment or account problems, email it@northstar.example.

## Account security

Activate your company account and enroll in multifactor authentication before accessing internal systems. Use the supplied security key as your primary authentication method.

Complete security training within three business days of your start date. Employees must finish training before receiving access to customer data. Your manager must also approve customer-data access; completing training alone does not grant access.

Never share passwords or authentication codes. Report a lost laptop or security key to IT immediately.

## Your first week

Meet with your manager to agree on three goals for your first 30 days. Record the goals in your onboarding checklist by the end of your fifth business day.

Your onboarding buddy schedules two check-ins during your first week. Each check-in lasts 20 minutes.

Employees working remotely may request a one-time home-office allowance of up to $250. Obtain written manager approval before purchasing eligible items, keep itemized receipts, and submit the reimbursement request within 30 calendar days of purchase. Eligible items are a keyboard, mouse, monitor, and desk lamp. Desks, chairs, and internet bills are not covered by this allowance.

## Your first month

Your manager schedules a 30-day review to discuss your onboarding goals and any remaining access or training needs. People Operations checks that your onboarding checklist is complete before closing it.

## Example

Morgan starts on Monday, March 2, 2026. Morgan must complete security training within the first three business days and obtain manager approval before receiving customer-data access. Morgan wants a $180 monitor for a home office. The monitor is eligible for the allowance, but Morgan must obtain written approval before buying it and submit an itemized receipt within 30 calendar days of the purchase.
