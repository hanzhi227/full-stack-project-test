# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
Next.js App Router and TypeScript, as approved in BUILD_PLAN.md.
## Users
Employees looking for policy answers in internal handbooks or guides.
## Product Purpose
Upload documents and ask questions; verify answers against cited source passages. Success is a real browser journey using embeddings, Zilliz and a language model.
## Operating Context
Small interview/demo application; fictional, non-sensitive documents only. Desktop workspace and mobile browser.
## Capabilities and Constraints
UTF-8 TXT/Markdown; 1 MB each; five documents per browser workspace. Signed cookie isolation. Six previous conversation messages. Bounded request, generation and grounding guards. No accounts, PDF support, streaming or persistent chat.
## Brand Commitments
Restrained typography and clear contrast. Exact copy and two-panel layout in BUILD_PLAN.md are binding.
## Evidence on Hand
BUILD_PLAN.md is the approved brief. No measured business benefits or customer claims.
## Accessibility & Inclusion
Visible keyboard focus, input labels, responsive layout and assistive-technology announcements.
